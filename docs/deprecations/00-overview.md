Massdriver removes deprecated features on their end-of-life date. After that date, the feature stops working. Migrate before the end-of-life date.

| Feature | End of life | Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| [Service account secrets](/deprecations/service-account-secrets) | December 31, 2026 | Access tokens |
